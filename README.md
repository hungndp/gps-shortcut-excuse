The GPS's excuse, in its own voice, for sending the driver down the wrong road, for make.fun's "The GPS Swears It Was a Shortcut" bounty ($2 in USDC).

The line `main.ts` writes to `/output/entry.txt` (192 characters, no trailing newline):

> In my defence, your honour, the map swore that lake was a road, the road swore it was a shortcut, and you said 'just trust the GPS' — so this was a group project, and I was only the intern.
